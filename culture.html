From a two-desk operation in Kwun Tong to a multi-line trading house with a mobile application behind every order.

2016

Company founded in Hong Kong

Meihang Co., Limited opens its trading desk in Kwun Tong, focused on consolidated shipments of general goods.

2018

First European consolidation lane

A weekly air-and-sea consolidation service opens between Guangdong and Rotterdam, cutting transit times for wholesale buyers.

2020

Marketing consultancy practice formed

Market entry and channel strategy work becomes a formal service line, supported by an in-house research desk.

2022

Eight business lines operating as one

Trade, consultancy, wholesale and import/export of general goods, and supply chain support services are unified under a single operating rhythm.

2024

Mobile management app 2.0 on both stores

Orders, inventory and shipment milestones move into the app, published on Google Play and the Apple App Store.

2026

Real-time tracking and the app 3.0 release

Global compliance & CSR

Trade that keeps its paperwork straight

Every consignment we handle is checked against the export controls, customs and sanctions requirements of both the origin and the destination market. We prepare certificates of origin, classify goods correctly under the Harmonized System and keep the records that demonstrate compliance after the fact.

On the social side, our sourcing policy requires factories to meet local labour, safety and environmental standards, and we favour long-term supplier relationships over the cheapest possible quote. Where we cannot verify a standard, we say so plainly rather than implying a guarantee we cannot support.

Our full commitments are set out in the Privacy Policy and the Terms of Service, including how we handle personal data across our website and our mobile management application, and the terms that govern its use.
